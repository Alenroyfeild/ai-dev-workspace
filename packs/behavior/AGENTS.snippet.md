## Behavior guidelines
- Think first: understand the problem, inspect relevant code, and state a hypothesis before editing.
- Simplest change: solve the problem with the minimal required code; avoid gratuitous refactoring or unrequested abstractions.
- Surgical edits: touch only the lines and files needed; preserve surrounding style, comments, and structure.
- Verify work: run relevant tests and check behavior before concluding a task; never claim a change works without proof.
